This is the SiliconNT project's fork of m1n1. Its work is on the `siliconnt/*` branches; `main` follows AsahiLinux/m1n1.

When changing upstream's files, keep to upstream's style and keep the change small.

Upstream (AsahiLinux/m1n1) does not accept contributions made with AI or LLM tools: https://asahilinux.org/slop/. Nothing from this fork is sent upstream. Pull requests go to SiliconNT/m1n1 (with `gh`, pass `--repo SiliconNT/m1n1`), never to AsahiLinux/m1n1.
